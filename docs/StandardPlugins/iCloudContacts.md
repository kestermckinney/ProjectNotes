# iCloud Contacts

Contact synchronization is built into Project Notes. On macOS, open **Settings → Preferences → Email, Contact and Calendar Integration**, choose **MacOS Email and Calendar**, and enter your Apple Account and app-specific password. Use **Test Connection** to discover address books, then enable synchronization.

See [Contact Synchronization](../InterfaceOverview/Preferences.md#contact-synchronization) for scheduling, optional export, fuzzy matching, review decisions, and recovery. Credentials remain in the operating system credential store. New configurations are paused until enabled; legacy account settings and valid identity links are migrated where available.

Photos, postal addresses, notes, groups, and list membership are outside the mapped contact fields. Remote deletions do not delete Project Notes people.
