# iCloud Contacts

Contact synchronization is built into Project Notes. Open **Settings → Email, Contacts, and Calendar**, choose **iCloud**, and enter your Apple Account and an app-specific password generated at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/). Use **Test Connection** to discover address books, then enable synchronization. **iCloud** is available on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

On macOS only, there's a second way to sync with Apple's contacts: choosing **MacOS Email and Calendar** instead reads and writes the Contacts app on this Mac directly, with no Apple Account or password to enter — see [Preferences — Contact Synchronization](../InterfaceOverview/Preferences.md#contact-synchronization) for that option. The rest of this page is about the **iCloud** choice specifically.

See [Contact Synchronization](../InterfaceOverview/Preferences.md#contact-synchronization) for scheduling, the separate Full Contact Export action, matching, review decisions, and recovery. Credentials remain in the operating system credential store. New configurations are paused until enabled; legacy account settings and valid identity links are migrated where available.

Photos, postal addresses, notes, groups, and list membership are outside the mapped contact fields. Remote deletions do not delete Project Notes contacts.
