# Find and Replace in Notes

The note editor includes an inline Find and Replace bar for locating and changing text within the note body without leaving the page.

**To open Find and Replace:**

1. Click into the **Note** field on a project note.
2. Press **Ctrl+F**, or click the **Find / Replace** label above the note editor.

The bar appears directly above the note, with a **Find** row and a **Replace** row. Closing it (its **X** button, or pressing **Ctrl+F** again) hides it without discarding your note edits.

## Finding Text

**To find text:**

1. Type the text you're looking for into the **Find** field. If you already had text selected in the note, it is filled in automatically.
2. Click **Next** (or press **Enter**) to jump to the next match, or **Previous** (or **Shift+Enter**) to jump to the one before.
3. Toggle the **Aa** button to make the search case-sensitive; by default matching ignores case.

The search wraps around: pressing **Next** from the last match jumps back to the first, and **Previous** from the first jumps to the last. If no match exists, the bar shows **Not found**. Each match found is selected and scrolled into view in the note.

## Replacing Text

**To replace text:**

1. Enter the text to search for in the **Find** field.
2. Enter its replacement in the **Replace with** field.
3. Click **Replace** to change the currently selected match and advance to the next one, or click **Replace All** to change every occurrence in the note at once.

After **Replace All**, the bar reports how many replacements were made. Replaced text inherits the formatting already present at that point in the note, so bold, italic, and other formatting are preserved around the change.
